Remote desktop, terminal and files

Help users and fix computers from the browser: no software on your side, no open ports on theirs. Everything goes through the agent's encrypted connection and is written to the audit log.

Remote desktop

Open a device and click Remote desktop (or open it in its own window). You see the screen and control it with mouse and keyboard.

  • Keyboard: click the screen to type; keys go to the remote computer with its own keyboard layout. Send Ctrl+Alt+Del and Send text (types a text, for example a long password) are in the toolbar. In full screen, shortcuts such as Ctrl+W or the Windows key also go to the remote computer; hold Esc to leave full screen.
  • Quality: Fast, Balanced or Sharp, depending on the connection.
  • Monitors: choose one monitor or all of them.
  • Sign-in and UAC screens are shown and can be used, so you can sign in and confirm administrator prompts.

Clipboard and files

On Windows computers, copied text goes both ways with Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V. Files copied in your Explorer are pasted on the remote computer with Ctrl+V. Files copied on the remote computer are offered as a download (Download copied files), because a browser cannot put files on your clipboard. The clipboard exchange can be switched off under Settings → Remote desktop.

Notice on the remote screen

By default the person at the computer sees a bar at the top of the screen with your name while you are connected. You do not see the bar in the picture. It can be switched off under Settings → Remote desktop; every session is written to the audit log either way.

Linux

Remote desktop works on Linux desktops with X11 (Xorg). Wayland does not let a background service see or control the screen: sign in with an X11 session there, or use the terminal. Without a graphical desktop, use the terminal.

Terminal (Linux)

Terminal opens a root shell of the device in the browser. Click into it to type; copy by selecting text, paste with Ctrl+Shift+V or Send text. The start and the end of the session are written to the audit log. On Windows, use Scripts.

Files

Files browses the drives of the computer without connecting to the screen. Upload files (up to 4 GB), download files, download a folder or a selection as ZIP, create folders, rename and delete. The agent does every file operation as SYSTEM (Windows) or root (Linux); every operation is written to the audit log with your name.

Processes and services

Processes lists running programs with their memory, threads and session and ends a process. Services lists services (Windows services or systemd units) and starts, stops or restarts them. The ControlZit agent protects itself: it cannot be stopped or ended from the console.
