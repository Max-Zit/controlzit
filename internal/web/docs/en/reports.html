Reports

A report shows a customer's devices over a period: operating systems, updates, alerts and the work of your technicians. Print it, save it as PDF or send it to the customer every week or month.

Creating a report

Under Reports choose a customer (or all customers) and the period (last 7, 30 or 90 days) and click Show report. It contains:

  • Devices — how many, online now, not seen for over 7 days, and the operating systems.
  • Updates — missing updates and missing security updates per device, devices waiting for a restart, update installations in the period.
  • Alerts — alerts opened in the period, how many were resolved and how fast, and what is still open.
  • Security and health — vulnerabilities with a fix (critical and high) and the programs that have them, devices with a disk or RAID problem, and network checks failing now.
  • Technician work — remote desktop and terminal sessions, scripts run and file operations, from the audit log.

Print or save as PDF uses the browser's print dialog; the menu and buttons are left out of the printout.

Reports by email

Under Settings → Reports every customer can get its report by email:

  1. Choose every week (last 7 days, on a weekday) or every month (last 30 days, on a day of the month), the time (server time) and the language of the report.
  2. Enter the recipients of each customer (addresses separated by commas, at most 20). Always send a copy to adds your own address to every customer's report.
  3. Send next to a customer sends its report now, to test it.

Email reports need the email server from Settings → Monitoring and alerts. The report is in the HTML part of the email.
