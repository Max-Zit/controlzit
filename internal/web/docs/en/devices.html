Customers, sites and devices

Devices are organised by customer and site. Technicians and read-only users can be limited to some customers, so every report and every list shows only what they may see.

Customers and sites

  • Customers are the companies you look after (or the departments of your own company). Add them under Customers.
  • Sites are their offices or branches. Every device belongs to one site; enrollment codes are created per site.
  • A customer or site can be deleted only when it no longer contains sites or devices.

The device list

Devices lists all devices you may see with their customer and site, operating system, the signed-in user, whether they are online and when they were last seen. Search by name, user, system or customer, or filter by customer and state.

Activity

Every action of the console on a device is a task: scripts, library jobs, Windows Update searches and installations, installing and uninstalling programs (winget, Chocolatey, the software library), agent updates, restart and shutdown, also the automatic ones of the server. The Activity tab of a device shows what is running, what is waiting and the history with the result and who started it; the number in the tab counts the running and waiting tasks. A task's name links to its details, for example the output of a script.

The tasks of a device run one after the other. If the device is offline, a task waits until it connects, up to 24 hours. You can leave the page at any time: a quick task shows its result at once, a longer one keeps running and the Activity tab updates by itself. Cancel removes a waiting task; Stop ends a running one: the agent stops the script or installer it runs for it, including the processes they started. Windows itself may still finish the update it is installing at that moment.

Inventory

The agent sends its inventory a few seconds after it connects and then regularly: operating system and build, domain or workgroup, model and serial number, processor, memory, disks with free space and BitLocker state, network adapters with addresses and gateways, Microsoft Defender and its signatures, the Windows Firewall profiles, a pending restart and the installed software (programs on Windows, packages on Linux). Refresh inventory asks for a new one at once.

Restart and shutdown

The power buttons on the device page restart or shut down the computer, now or after a delay, with an optional message to the signed-in user; a scheduled restart can be cancelled. Users signed in there lose unsaved work.

Removing a device

Remove device deletes it from the console. An online agent uninstalls itself; see Removing an agent.
